A study of what Blender can do for architectural design and realistic interiors, rendered with Cycles. A good interior render needs everyday things to look lived-in, so I also modeled the furniture: table, chairs, cabinets, oven, refrigerator and more. Then I rendered the kitchen twice, in daylight and at night, when a papier-mâché chandelier takes over.
