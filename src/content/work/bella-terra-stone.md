The client asked for two directions for a company that designs and manufactures interiors, much of it in natural stone. They wanted something with weight: a mark that reads like an old house crest, not a startup.

Both directions are heraldic. The first is a shield with crossed swords and a mountain peak; the second puts the initials in a crest held by two lions, with stone columns and a crown. Each is drawn as clean vectors and delivered in every lockup the brand needs: square, circle seal with the slogan "A heart of stone for your home", horizontal, light and dark, plus print mockups and guidelines for using them.
