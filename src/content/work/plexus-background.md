A 3D scene of glowing plexus elements: nodes that drift through space and link to their neighbours with thin threads, with a shallow depth of field so near and far points melt into soft light. Rendered as a looping video for backgrounds.

The net behind this site is a real-time version of the same idea, rebuilt in Three.js so it reacts to the page you are on.
