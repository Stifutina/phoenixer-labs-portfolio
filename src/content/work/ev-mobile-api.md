A mobile app for designing modifications for electric vehicles: colors, wheels, wraps, then a high-resolution photo of the result.

## What I built

- **User authorization** with OAuth providers.
- **Saving and updating user designs**, with server-side image processing.
- **A like and dislike system** and **achievements** to keep people coming back.
- **Input validation** on every endpoint, because mobile clients send everything.
