A looping video background of a natural landscape for a Framer website. The animation is the point: wind sways the grass across the hills, slow enough to sit behind text and real enough to hold attention.

I modeled the terrain, scattered and animated the grass, lit the scene for that early-evening teal and pink, and rendered a seamless loop. Then I set it into the client's Framer site, with separate cuts for wide and vertical screens so the composition holds on every device.
