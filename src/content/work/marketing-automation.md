A large e-commerce platform that helps online stores turn visitors into buyers with targeted banners, forms and email. I led the backend and was responsible for code quality across the team.

## What I built

- **A WYSIWYG editor backend** for promotional banners and forms that merchants design in the browser and embed on any website.
- **Integrations with mailing services**: Mailchimp, SendGrid, HubSpot and others.
- **A scheduling algorithm** for promotional messages that fires at the right local time in any timezone.
- **Website integrations** that let the product run on almost any storefront.

The stack around it: MongoDB, Elasticsearch, Redis and Docker, running on AWS and Google Cloud. The client is under NDA, so the screen shown here is representative, not the product itself.
