Forty scenes built while going deep into Three.js: lighting and shadows, PBR materials, physics, glTF models, custom GLSL shaders and GPU particles (a galaxy of a million points, fireworks, a flow field).

I also designed and coded the home page: a filterable gallery where every card opens its scene in one click, and a starfield flying past in the background.

It's a good preview of what I build for clients: 3D product viewers, landing pages with real-time graphics, animated web visuals that stay fast.
