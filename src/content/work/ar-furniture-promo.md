An idea for a furniture manufacturer's promo video in an augmented-reality style: the product sits in real footage as if it were there, then the balloons lift it over the city.

The scene is built so its elements can be changed later without starting over: replace the sofa with another product, change the letters on the balloons, add a discount slogan. One setup, many campaigns.
