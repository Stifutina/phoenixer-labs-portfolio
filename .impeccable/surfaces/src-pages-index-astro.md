## Direction contract

THESIS: Two lights in one sky. The starfield already holds ice and amber stars; ice is Sergey, amber is Anastasia. Navigating into a wing warps the sky and brightens that person's stars. Refuses the developer-portfolio default: centered hero, skills icon grid, row of equal project cards.

OWN-WORLD: Night navy ground (#060812) with deep radial skies, Bricolage Grotesque throughout, hairline lavender rules, ice #8fd3ff and amber #ffc46b as the only accents, each owning its wing. Cards are dark translucent panels with a cursor-following gradient rim and a slight 3D tilt.

STORY: The visitor learns this is two seniors covering backend, AI and 3D, understands who does what, sees proof, and opens the contact page from wherever they are.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left-weighted headline across ~8 of 12 columns at display scale; the two people as two lit "door" panels stacked lower right, each glowing in its own light with the cursor-lit rim, linking into the wings. Top bar: wordmark left, wing links, and a persistent "Start a project" pill top right (fixed bottom pill on mobile).
